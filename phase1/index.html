Day 2 · Mon 16 Aug

Pravčická brána + Edmund's Gorge boats

20 km · 34 min driving Apartmány Jožák, Arnoltice → Tři prameny (Pravčická brána trailhead) → Hřensko (Edmund's Gorge boats) → Apartmány Jožák, Arnoltice Open route in Google Maps

Driving notes by country

Czech Republic

  • Motorway e-vignette required. 2026 price: 10 days 300 CZK, 30 days 480 CZK. Rental cars registered in Czechia normally have one – check at pick-up.
  • Children under 36 kg AND under 150 cm must use a child restraint, so both kids need one. High-back boosters suit ages 5 and 7. A backless booster is allowed only from 125 cm and 22 kg. Fine: 1,500–2,000 CZK.
  • Headlights on day and night. Zero alcohol (0.0‰). Speed limits: 50 km/h in towns, 90 outside, 130 on motorways.
  • Hands-free phone only. A reflective vest must be in the car. Trams have priority in Prague.
  • Prague: leave the car at the hotel (free parking) and use metro/trams. Blue street zones are for residents only.
  • Petrol ≈ €1.85/l (≈ 45 CZK, Oct 2026). Most stations are self-service and take cards.

Germany (Day 3, Bastei)

  • No vignette or toll for cars.
  • Children under 12 and under 150 cm need a child seat.
  • Bastei: paid car park near the bridge. A park-and-ride with a shuttle bus is used when the main park is full.

Austria (only if you detour)

  • Digital vignette 10 days €12.80. Children under 14 and under 135 cm need a child seat.
