Pick-up at the Zurich Airport car rental centre after the 21:35 landing on Sat 14 Aug (about 22:15–22:30); return there on Mon 23 Aug by about 09:30 = 9 rental days. Desk hours found: Sixt and Enterprise 06:00–23:30 daily, Avis and Hertz listed 06:00/06:30–23:30 (third-party) – book a supplier that confirms the pick-up against your flight number. Use in Germany: Enterprise/National say no cross-border charge; Sixt allows Germany ('Zone 1') with a possible fee; Europcar's terms were not found.   • Border: the only crossings are the airport drives. In: Zurich Airport → Swiss A51 (Kloten–Bülach) and main road 4 → Rafz, into Germany at Rafz–Lottstetten (Solgen) and through the Jestetten–Wutach area to Schluchsee (70 km / 1h11). Germany has run temporary checks at its land borders since 2023–24: carry passports and expect short delays.
  • Switzerland (short airport legs): motorway vignette required on the A51, A3, A1 and A11 (CHF 40, fine CHF 200 without it); speed limits 50 km/h in towns, 80 outside, 100 on expressways, 120 on motorways – cameras are everywhere and fines are steep (radar detectors are illegal). Dipped headlights or daytime running lights at all times; alcohol limit 0.5‰ (0.1‰ for new drivers – in practice, do not drink); children under 12 and under 150 cm in an approved child seat or booster; phones hands-free only; warning triangle in the car.
  • Child seats: in Germany children under 12 and shorter than 150 cm must use an approved child seat or booster (ECE/EU standard); a rear-facing seat may not go in front of an active airbag. Request 2 boosters when booking.
  • Speed: 50 km/h in towns, 100 outside, no general limit on the Autobahn but 130 is recommended and many sections are signed lower; Black Forest roads are winding – plan by OSRM times plus 15%. Alcohol limit 0.5‰ (0.0 for drivers under 21 or with under 2 years' licence). Phones hands-free only. Warning triangle, first-aid kit and vests are in the car. No motorway tolls for cars in Germany.
