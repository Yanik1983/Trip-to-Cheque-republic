Car (Czech Republic only)

Pick-up Prague Airport Thu 19 Aug 10:30, return Tue 24 Aug by 10:30 (5 days; most companies allow about 29–59 min grace, so check the terms). Category: intermediate/standard estate, e.g. Škoda Octavia Combi automatic. 2 large suitcases + 2 trolleys fit in the boot, with 2 high-back boosters on the rear seat. Confirm the Czech e-vignette is included.

  • Prague without a car: kids up to 15 ride free on Prague public transport as far as I know (verify); adults buy a 72-hour ticket (330 CZK). Taxis: book ones with child seats, or bring your 2 boosters (you'll need them in the car anyway).
  • Czech motorway e-vignette: normally included with Czech rental cars (10 days 300 CZK if not).
  • Children under 36 kg AND under 150 cm must sit in a child seat. High-back boosters suit ages 5 and 7.
  • Headlights on day and night. Zero alcohol. Speed limits 50 / 90 / 130 km/h.
  • Petrol ≈ €1.85/l (Oct 2026).
